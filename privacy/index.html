Privacy Policy

LacyDaily1 · last updated September 30, 2026

LacyDaily1 is a single-operator personal tool. It does not collect data from, or about, the public.

What data this tool touches

  • OAuth tokens for the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts, used solely to upload videos the operator has personally reviewed and approved. These tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device and are never shared with a third party.
  • Public stream footage (video and chat log) from a publicly broadcast VOD, used only to identify and render highlight clips.

What this tool does not do

  • Does not collect, store, or process data belonging to viewers, chat participants, or any third party.
  • Does not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties beyond the YouTube/TikTok APIs needed to publish a clip the operator approved.
  • Does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

Platform data

Where this tool connects to YouTube or TikTok, each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle the resulting account data.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the tool's functionality changes.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the tool's operator.
